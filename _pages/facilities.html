Synchrotron & Neutron Facilities

Synchrotron facilities

  • European Synchrotron Radiation Facility (ESRF)

    Grenoble, France

    • BM01 (SNBL I) — Diffraction of single crystals, powders and thin films
    • BM31 (SNBL II) — In situ and operando XAS, XRD and PDF
    • ID22 — High-resolution, high-flux powder diffraction

  • MAX IV

    Lund, Sweden

    • HIPPIE — In situ and operando ambient-pressure XPS (AP-XPS)
    • Balder — In situ and operando hard X-ray absorption and emission spectroscopy (XAS/XES)

  • Diamond Light Source

    Didcot, UK

    • I14 — In situ nano-XAS and nano-XRF
    • I15-1 — High-energy X-ray pair distribution function measurements (XPDF)

  • Advanced Light Source (ALS)

    Berkeley, USA

    Photocatalytic experiments

  • Shanghai Synchrotron Radiation Facility (SSRF)

    Shanghai, China

    • BL02B — Soft X-ray ambient-pressure photoelectron spectroscopy (AP-XPS)

Spallation neutron facilities

Neutron diffraction and neutron pair distribution function (nPDF) measurements.

  • Spallation Neutron Source (SNS)

    Oak Ridge National Laboratory, USA

    NOMAD and POWGEN

  • China Spallation Neutron Source (CSNS)

    Dongguan, China

    Multi-Physics Instrument (MPI)

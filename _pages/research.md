My research connects atomic-scale structure and defect chemistry to the properties of functional materials. At the University of Oslo, I focus on how oxide electrodes and electrolytes respond to changes in temperature and gas atmosphere.

## Nanoparticle exsolution in perovskites {#nanoparticle-exsolution}

Metal nanoparticles can precipitate from a perovskite during reduction, creating interfaces relevant to electrocatalysis. I investigate how point defects and host composition influence when these particles form and where they grow. In situ diffraction and X-ray absorption spectroscopy help track changes in the host oxide and the transition metals during reduction.

Related papers cover [the role of point defects in exsolution]({{ '/publications/exsolution-point-defects/' | relative_url }}) and [the stages of nanoparticle formation]({{ '/publications/multiple-stages-of-metal-exsolution/' | relative_url }}).

## Hydration and defects in proton ceramics {#proton-ceramics}

I study how dopants, oxygen vacancies and extended defects influence proton uptake in ceramic electrolytes and electrodes. In acceptor-doped barium zirconate, for example, nickel can alter the local defect environment and reduce hydration. I combine local structural measurements with atomistic calculations to connect these changes to the behaviour of the bulk ceramic.

See our [study of nickel-induced defects in barium zirconate]({{ '/publications/nickel-induced-lattice-defects/' | relative_url }}).

## Local structure in functional materials {#local-structure}

Local atomic arrangements can differ from the average crystal structure. I use neutron and synchrotron X-ray total scattering and pair distribution function (PDF) analysis, alongside density functional theory (DFT) and *ab initio* molecular dynamics (AIMD), to study disorder and nanoscale distortions in materials such as ferroelectric perovskites and thermoelectric SnSe.

See the studies on [disordered Bi₀.₅K₀.₅TiO₃]({{ '/publications/quasirandom-structures-bkt/' | relative_url }}) and [the structural phase transition in SnSe]({{ '/publications/structural-phase-transition-snse/' | relative_url }}).
